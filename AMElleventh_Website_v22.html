How we manage delivery

What protects you when the team is offshore

Outsourcing goes wrong when nobody owns quality. Here is how we own it.

Partner selection+
Delivery partners are vetted before they take on work: their facilities, management, hiring standards and track record. Each one signs our service agreement and works to our rate card.
Quality monitoring+
Every programme has agreed targets, such as response times, accuracy and resolution. We review them weekly with the partner, and you get a summary every month.
Escalation+
Your account lead in Dubai is your single point of escalation. If a target slips, we agree a fix with the partner and tell you what's being done and by when.
Continuity+
Your processes are written down and backup staff are trained, so absences and staff turnover don't stop your operation. If a partner underperforms, we can move the work to another one.
Data and confidentiality+
Confidentiality and data-handling terms are written into both your contract and the partner agreement. Access to your systems is limited to the roles that need it, and the controls are agreed with you during scoping.

Is 30 seats the minimum?+
It's where most pilots start, because that's enough to set up proper supervision and quality control. If you need fewer people, book a scoping call. We'll tell you honestly whether we can do it well or whether another option suits you better.
How is pricing set?+
Each role is priced on its own. You get the full rate card in your written proposal after the scoping call.
Who does the work?+
Vetted delivery partners, mostly in the Philippines, working under our service agreements. Your contract, your account lead and our accountability all stay with AMElleventh in the UAE.
Can the team cover UAE business hours?+
Yes. Manila is four hours ahead of Dubai, and shifts are set around the hours you need, including evenings and weekends.
How quickly can a pilot start?+
It depends on the roles and volumes. Your proposal includes a start date and a ramp-up plan once scoping is done.
