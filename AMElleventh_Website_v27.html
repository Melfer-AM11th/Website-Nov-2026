How we manage delivery

What protects you when the team is offshore

Outsourcing goes wrong when nobody owns quality. Here is how we own it.

Partner selection+
Delivery partners are vetted before they take on work: their facilities, management, hiring standards and track record. Each one signs our service agreement and works to our rate card.
Quality monitoring+
Every programme has agreed targets, such as response times, accuracy and resolution. We review them weekly with the partner, and you get a summary every month.
Escalation+
Your account lead in Dubai is your single point of escalation. If a target slips, we agree a fix with the partner and tell you what's being done and by when.
Continuity+
Your processes are written down and backup staff are trained, so absences and staff turnover don't stop your operation. If a partner underperforms, we can move the work to another one.
Data, confidentiality and compliance+
Confidentiality and data-handling terms are written into both your contract and the partner agreement. Access to your systems is limited to the roles that need it. For sales and collections programmes, we build training and monitoring around the rules of the bank or regulator you represent, and the controls are agreed with you during scoping.

Questions

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Send us a WhatsApp message.

Is 30 seats the minimum?+
It's where most pilots start, because that's enough to set up proper supervision and quality control. If you need fewer people, book a scoping call. We'll tell you honestly whether we can do it well or whether another option suits you better.
Can you build bank-product sales and collections teams?+
Yes. We build sales teams for cards, loans and mortgages, and leasing and collections teams for real estate firms, working to your scripts and targets. The product rules and approvals required by your principal bank or regulator stay the reference, and we build training and quality monitoring around them.
How is pricing set?+
Each role is priced on its own. You get the full rate card in your written proposal after the scoping call.
Do you serve clients in Saudi Arabia and the rest of the GCC?+
Yes. Your business can be anywhere in the GCC. Your contract, account lead and reporting stay in Dubai, and we choose the delivery hub that suits you. During scoping we confirm any requirements specific to your market, such as language, local hiring and data handling.
Can you deliver from India or an Arab country?+
Yes, by programme. The Philippines is our core hub, and we work in India and the Arab region through vetted delivery partners when language, skills or cost call for it. Tell us what you need and we'll recommend the best fit, honestly.
Who does the work?+
Vetted delivery partners in the Philippines, India or the Arab region depending on the programme, working under our service agreements. Your contract, your account lead and our accountability all stay with AMElleventh in the UAE.
Can the team cover my business hours?+
Yes. Shifts are set around the hours you need in the UAE, Saudi Arabia or any GCC market, including evenings and weekends. Manila is four hours ahead of Dubai, India is one and a half hours ahead, and Saudi Arabia is one hour behind.
How quickly can a pilot start?+
It depends on the roles and volumes. Your proposal includes a start date and a ramp-up plan once scoping is done.
